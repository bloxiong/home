## Platform

web

## Users

The marketing site for Bloxio Nigeria Limited serves four audiences, all confirmed by the founders:

- **Farmers and agribusiness operators** (Nigeria first) who lose yield to late-detected disease, soil problems and weather, and who may join the AgroSense360 pilot / early-access list.
- **Businesses that need engineering work**: R&D, product design and manufacturing, IoT/smart-device systems, technical consulting, support.
- **Investors and partners** evaluating whether to back or partner with an early-stage Nigerian hardware company.
- **Talent and the general public**: engineers who might join, press, curious visitors.

Many visitors arrive on mobile, on Nigerian networks.

## Product Purpose

Bloxio is an early-stage, two-founder engineering company in Lagos that researches, designs and builds hardware and software products (AI, IoT, electronics). The site must make the company legible and credible as a real business: what it builds, what stage it is at, who runs it, and how each audience engages.

## Positioning

An engineer-founded Nigerian company building its own hardware products from the ground up, starting with agriculture (AgroSense360), while offering the same engineering capability as a service.

## Product Principles

1. Say exactly where things stand. Prototype means prototype.
2. Every audience gets a clear next step within one click.
3. Spectacle never blocks information: motion is fast, skippable, and off on small screens.
4. Built for Nigerian conditions first: light pages, mobile-first, works on slow networks.
